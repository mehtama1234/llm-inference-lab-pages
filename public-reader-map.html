fast route

Read five pages if you want the whole argument quickly.

This route is for a reader who wants the end-to-end problem, the mathematical reason the techniques work, the experiment discipline, and the final decision gate without reading every microscope page.

1. Current statuscurrent-status.html gives the one-page state: done, locked, and next host proof.
2. Synthesisend-to-end-synthesis.html explains the overall chain from model arithmetic to serving decision.
3. Source mathsource-math-deep-dives.html explains why the main papers and systems work.
4. Paper atlaspaper-mechanism-atlas.html goes deeper per source family: object changed, equation, failure boundary, and experiment proof.
5. Derivation workbookmechanism-derivation-workbook.html derives the inequalities that decide when an optimization should move a metric.
6. Proof sketchesproof-sketches.html gives the compact mathematical validity checks.
7. Mechanism mapmechanism-experiment-deep-map.html connects source math to falsifiable lab runs.
8. Experiment designexperiment-design-calculus.html turns mechanisms into controlled runs.
9. Capstone gatecapstone-readiness-checklist.html says when a recommendation is allowed.
10. Publication outlinecapstone-publication-outline.html assembles the final article and marks the empty host-evidence slot.
11. Claim ledgerpublic-claim-ledger.html lists which public conclusions are allowed, bounded, or locked.
12. Completion auditpublication-completion-audit.html states what is done, what is locked, and when the capstone can be marked complete.
13. Finish linecapstone-finish-line.html names the host return and five reporting updates that finish the capstone.
Target artifactcapstone-case-file.html is the concrete draft of the final public report.
