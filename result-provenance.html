claim rule

Provenance controls what a result is allowed to say.

The audit lesson is simple: every chart should show whether the numbers are real GPU measurements, local simulations, deterministic teaching traces, source-backed studies, runtime probes, or local validation artifacts. Otherwise a reader can mistake setup evidence for speed evidence.

Real measurementCan support a bounded empirical claim for that model, workload, GPU, dependency set, and run condition.
Mixed recordCan explain a measured result with derived math, but the derived part must stay visible.
Validation contractCan prove the comparison machinery fails closed or accepts shaped inputs; it is not a benchmark.
Runbook or planCan prove readiness, prerequisites, and proof limits; it cannot supply latency or throughput.
