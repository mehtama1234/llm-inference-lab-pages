math claim dependency graph

A public inference claim is a path from an equation to a measured gate.

This graph connects the lab's first-principles math, paper mechanisms, returned experiments, and locked production claims. It is meant to answer one question cleanly: what has to be true mathematically before an experiment result is allowed to become a public conclusion?

dependency rule

The dependency chain is premise, mechanism, measurement, boundary, claim.

A benchmark number is not self-explaining. The math says which term should move. The experiment says whether it moved under controlled conditions. The claim is allowed only when the measured movement matches the mathematical reason and the boundary is still the same.

claim_allowed = mathematical_premise_true and mechanism_preserves_task and changed_variable_isolated and measured_metric_moves_as_predicted and non_claim_is_explicit production_winner_allowed = claim_allowed and same_model and same_tokenizer and same_request_file and same_host and same_client and both_runtime_contracts_pass and fair_gate_passes
PremiseThe equation that says where cost, error, or capacity comes from.
MechanismThe paper or runtime trick that changes one term in that equation.
MeasurementThe artifact that proves the term moved under the lab boundary.
LockThe claim that stays unavailable until the missing gate returns.
dependency rows

Each major subtheme has a different mathematical gate.

Attention scaling premise
Var(q dot k) = d, so divide by sqrt(d) to keep score scale stable. mechanism
Transformer attention keeps token selection numerically comparable across head widths. measurement
Explains logits, entropy, and next-token behavior; not an optimization benchmark by itself. claim
Allowed as model-function math; no serving speed claim follows from it.
FlashAttention premise
Avoided O(n^2) intermediate memory traffic can dominate tiling overhead. mechanism
Compute exact attention while streaming tiles and maintaining softmax statistics. measurement
Should move memory bandwidth, kernel time, or long-context throughput under fixed sequence shape. claim
Allowed only when the workload exposes attention-memory pressure.
KV cache and paging premise
KV_bytes = 2 * layers * hidden * tokens * bytes grows with active tokens. mechanism
KV caching avoids recomputing history; paging reduces fragmentation and admission waste. measurement
vLLM pressure probes show slot, prompt, output, prefix, and streaming behavior. claim
Allowed as vLLM pressure mechanics; runtime winner remains locked.
Batching and queues premise
Latency contains service time plus waiting time; waiting rises sharply near saturation. mechanism
Continuous batching increases useful GPU work, but queue and memory limits create knees. measurement
Arrival pressure, active sequences, TTFT, completion latency, and rejection/error rows. claim
Allowed only inside the measured traffic distribution and concurrency range.
Quantization premise
Lower bytes reduce memory pressure, but quantization error perturbs activations and logits. mechanism
Use fewer bits for weights or adapters while keeping task error below the margin. measurement
Fit, latency, and quality must be reported separately. claim
Fit does not imply quality; quality does not imply production serving speed.
LoRA and Unsloth SFT premise
Delta W = A B constrains update rank so trainable parameters are much smaller than full weights. mechanism
Adapters change task behavior through a low-rank weight update. measurement
SFT adapter archive and reload probe showed a changed output on NVIDIA L4. claim
Allowed as portable adapter workflow; quality and serving speed need separate gates.
GRPO reasoning premise
Group-relative rewards can push probability mass toward higher-scoring completions without a value model. mechanism
Reward shape supplies a local direction for policy updates. measurement
Reward contract and mini-train passed; held-out quality delta was zero. claim
Training workflow allowed; durable reasoning improvement locked.
TGI versus vLLM premise
Runtime policy changes scheduling, KV management, queue wait, and decode cadence. mechanism
Two serving stacks may move TTFT, p95 latency, throughput, errors, and utilization differently. measurement
Needs returned same-host archive with both runtime contracts and the fair gate passing. claim
Production runtime winner locked until host return.
claim locks

The lock exists where the dependency chain is incomplete.

The lab already has enough evidence for mechanism learning. It does not yet have enough evidence for the final serving winner because the dependency chain ends at a missing host return archive.
