report shape

The article should make one hard promise: every conclusion is tied to its evidence boundary.

The report should not read like a catalog of tools. It should read like a proof chain: what problem are we solving, what mathematics creates the bottlenecks, what approaches target each bottleneck, what experiments returned, and what production decision is allowed.
