evidence rule

A claim must carry its mechanism, evidence boundary, measured movement, and non-claim.

The mathematical object in this lab is not just latency or throughput. It is a conditional statement: if the workload, model, hardware, and serving policy are fixed, then a mechanism may move one metric for a reason we can explain from memory traffic, arithmetic work, queueing, quantization error, or reward shape. Remove one condition and the conclusion becomes narrower.
